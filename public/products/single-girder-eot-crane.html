Single Girder EOT Crane

Single Girder EOT Cranes Manufacturer in India

Sahil Industries Single Girder cranes are modular in construction, compact and light weight design which ensures less building space, optimum utilization of floor space and ensure maximum rigidity for a minimum deadweight. This keeps the load on the crane runway low, and a cost-effective design can be selected for the building.

It consist of fabricated Standard sections or box type plate fabricated beam, wheels, gearbox, motors, and running overhead to lift materials and run at both axis.

Our Sigle Girder Electric EOT Cranes are Design & Manufacturing as per IS: 807, IS:3177

Standard Features

S.G. EOT Cranes and D.G. Modular EOT Cranes

  •  Standard Single Speed Motors
  •  Two Speed option available through variable frequence drives, Micro motor and two speed motors
  •  Compact Hoist design, giving minimum hook approaches
  •  Quality Test certificates for hoist test, wire rope, hook forgings etc.
  •  Electromagnetic break for hoisting motion
  •  Option of centrifugal mechanical/ AC or DC electromagnetic break for Cross and long travel motions.
  •  Welded box type beam or standard rolled 'I' beam construction for main bridge girder
  •  Standard Supply voltage 415V, 50 Hz
  •  Standard Control Voltage 110V
  •  Optional Control Voltages 24V/48V
  •  Standard Cross travel speed 15 mpm
  •  Standard Long travel speed 20 mpm
  •  Other speed available upon request
  •  Tables indicate Cranes designed as per Class Il as per IS 3177and IS 807
  •  Cranes can also be designed for other Classifications
  •  Compact long and cross travel drives
  •  Limit switches for all motions
  •  Emergency upper limit switch for hoisting motion
  •  Low headroom hoist option
  •  Underslung cranes can also be supplied where there is constraint of space
  •  Crane component kits can also be supplied

Common Optional Features for all Cranes

  •  Anti-collision arrangement for two or more cranes working in the same bay
  •  Independent moving pendent
  •  Load indicator
  •  Radio remote Control
  •  Alarm
  •  Bridge lights
