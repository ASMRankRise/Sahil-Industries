About Us

Hoist EOT Crances Manufacturer in India

Sahil Industries

Sahil Industries has it's manufacturing unit in Kolhapur's prestigious industrial area, five star MIDC, Kagal.

Our pre-engineered cranes are manufactured as per the IS guidelines and taking into considerations the particular need of our client. We are proud to see our cranes being established globally and internationally, handling a wide variety of materials. Our range of industrial cranes & components is available with lifting capacity of up to 200 tons and includes wire rope hoist or open winch design, as well as Explosion proof cranes, and a variety of crane components.

Similarly we have more than 100 clients to whom we have designed and provided solutions as per their unique needs, like repairing, maintenance and servicing support. We provide these solutions not only to the products we have manufactured, but any other product of any make, that our client has. Also our response time to the customer needs is phenomenal and that is why our clients keep coming back to us again and again.
